KEY FINDINGS FOR PAPER
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
 
Finding 1: Decision Axis is Principal Component
  - PC1 aligns with chord direction (cos = 0.75 ± 0.11)
  - Explains ~63% of variance on average
  - Implies model learns structured representation of the task
 
Finding 2: Orthogonal Encoding of Uncertainty
  - Orthogonal subspace variance peaks at ambiguous frame
  - Spearman correlation: ρ = -0.63 ± 0.18 (strongly peaks at center)
  - Suggests model learned to encode "confidence" separately from "decision"
 
Finding 3: Low-Dimensional Manifold
  - ~20x compression from original to effective dimensionality
  - 25-30 dimensions (vs 512-768 original) capture 95% variance
  - Implies 10-frame sequences trace smooth 25-30D curve through space
  
Finding 4: Curvature Concentration
  - Trajectory curvature ~13° on average (not straight)
  - Highest curvature within 1.5 frames of center in 70% of sequences
  - Explains why perpendicular distance method works
 
Finding 5: Cross-Architecture Consistency
  - All 4 models (LLaVA, Phi-4, Qwen, Pixtral) show similar structure
  - Differences exist but pattern holds universally
  - Suggests this is fundamental property of how LVLMs work, not artifact
